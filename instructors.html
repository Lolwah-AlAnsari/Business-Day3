Our standard

What “certified” means here

Pilates is an unregulated word. A weekend course and a six-hundred-hour comprehensive certification can both put it on a wall.

Everyone who teaches at Plié holds a full comprehensive certification — BASI, STOTT, Polestar or Balanced Body — covering mat and every piece of apparatus, with supervised teaching hours and examined practice. We fund continuing education for the whole team every year, and nobody teaches a specialism here without a specific qualification in it.

If you want to see a certificate, ask. We will happily show you.
