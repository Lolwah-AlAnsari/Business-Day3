Welcome to Plié

A small studio, on purpose

We opened Plié because the Pilates we loved kept getting bigger — more beds, more people, less teaching. So we built the opposite.

Every class here is capped low enough that your instructor can watch you move, put a hand on the rib that isn’t doing its job, and change the plan when your body says something different today. That is the whole idea. Not harder classes. Better attention.

Whether you have been practising for a decade or have never touched a reformer, you will be taught — properly, at your pace, in a room that feels calm from the moment you take your shoes off.
