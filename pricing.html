The small print

Good to know

  • Cancel free up to 12 hours before class and your credit returns immediately. Inside 12 hours the credit is spent — the bed was held for you.
  • Credits expire 30 days after a drop-in, two months after a 5-class pack, three months after a 10-class pack. Monthly Unlimited resets each billing month.
  • Freezes are available for travel or illness. Email us and we will usually pause your pack for up to a month.
  • No joining fee and no contract. Monthly Unlimited renews each month and can be cancelled any time before the next billing date.
  • Grip socks are required for reformer classes. We sell them at reception for 4 KWD if you forget.
