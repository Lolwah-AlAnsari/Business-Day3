About us

A room built to slow you down

Plié is a four-instructor studio in Salmiya with eight reformer beds and a stubborn belief that smaller classes teach better.

Our story

It started with a waiting list

In 2019 Noura Al-Rashid was teaching out of a borrowed room with two reformers and a waiting list she could not clear. People kept coming back for the same reason: it was the first time anyone had actually watched them move.

Four years, one long renovation and a great deal of paperwork later, that borrowed room became Plié. We took a villa on Salem Al Mubarak Street, pulled out the ceiling, laid a sprung floor, and fitted exactly eight reformers — two fewer than the space could hold, because ten would have meant teaching to a crowd.

Today four instructors teach thirty-five classes a week here. Some of the people from that first waiting list are still with us.

Our philosophy

Precision is the point

Joseph Pilates called it Contrology — the study of control. Not exhaustion, not sweat, not how many repetitions you survived.

We teach that literally. A class here is quieter than you might expect and slower than you might want. We will take a spring off before we add one. We will stop you at six repetitions if the seventh would be sloppy. When a shape isn’t working, we change the shape rather than asking you to push through it.

The result creeps up on people. You notice it carrying shopping, or on the third flight of stairs, or the morning you realise your lower back simply didn’t come up in conversation with itself.

The space

What it’s like inside

A converted villa across two floors, with a studio designed around how the light moves through it.

The studio floor

Eight reformers on a sprung timber floor, arranged in a shallow arc rather than rows. No mirrors down the long wall — we would rather you felt the movement than watched it.

The mat room

A quieter room upstairs with fourteen mats, warm underfloor heating and a wall of props: rings, bands, soft balls, bolsters and blocks.

Changing & showers

Two changing rooms with showers, lockers, towels and everything you need to go straight to work afterwards. Tea and cold water at reception, always.
