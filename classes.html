Your first visit

How a class actually runs

Step 01

Arrive early

Ten minutes before your first class. We will show you the space, take a quick note of any injuries, and set up your bed or mat with you.

Step 02

Warm up and breathe

The first ten minutes are spent finding your breath and switching on the deep core. It feels like very little. It is the most important part.

Step 03

Work through the sequence

A full-body sequence, cued out loud and adjusted individually. Your instructor will offer you an easier and a harder version of most things.

Step 04

Wind down

Five minutes of stretch and release. You leave taller than you arrived — that part is not marketing, it is decompression.
